# Fsp Art Direction — Stylized Realistic Battle Royale

## Identity
Original mobile-first battle royale identity. Do not copy PUBG names, logos, UI layouts, maps, characters, costumes, weapon skins, sounds, promotional art, or distinctive visual assets.

## Visual language
- Stylized-realistic 3D: believable proportions with simplified materials and geometry.
- Mediterranean / North-African inspired fictional world without using protected real-world brands.
- Warm sand, limestone and muted earth surfaces contrasted with deep navy UI and restrained bronze accents.
- Clean silhouettes readable on small Android screens.
- Avoid excessive military insignia, real manufacturer marks, and recognizable branded uniforms.

## Characters
- Original tactical-survivor silhouettes, not copies of existing battle-royale characters.
- Base outfit: layered field jacket/shirt, utility trousers, compact backpack, gloves/boots.
- Cosmetic slots: head, face, torso, legs, backpack, parachute.
- LOD0 target 25–35k triangles; LOD1 12–18k; LOD2 5–8k for hero characters.
- One shared humanoid skeleton where possible to reduce memory and animation cost.

## Animation set
Idle, walk, run, sprint, crouch, jump, freefall, parachute, aim, fire, reload, hit reaction, death, enter vehicle, drive, exit vehicle.

## Weapons
- Fictionalized/original weapon presentation and naming.
- Matte metal + polymer + restrained wear; no copied skins or logos.
- Clear category silhouettes: assault rifle, SMG, marksman, shotgun.
- Weapon-on-back sockets and hand sockets standardized across characters.

## World
- Fictional coastal/desert island.
- Districts: old stone town, port, dry farms, quarry, airfield, hill fort, industrial edge.
- Buildings use modular kits and repeated materials for performance.
- Strong landmark silhouettes for navigation without copying another game's map.

## HUD
- Original layout, not a PUBG recreation.
- Deep navy translucent panels, bronze highlights, off-white text.
- Large touch targets, safe-area aware, minimal screen obstruction.
- Circular minimap with our own frame/icons; compact health/armor/ammo cluster.

## Mobile performance targets
- 30 FPS baseline on modest Android devices; optional 60 FPS tier.
- Baked lighting where practical, one main realtime directional light.
- Texture atlases, ASTC on supported Android, aggressive LOD/culling.
- Avoid realtime reflections and large transparent particle overdraw.

## Store/IP checklist
- Only original or properly licensed assets, fonts, music and SFX.
- Keep source/license records for third-party assets.
- No competitor trademarks in title, icon, screenshots or description.
- Store screenshots must represent actual gameplay/UI.
