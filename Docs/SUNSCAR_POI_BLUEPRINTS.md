# Sunscar Island — POI Blueprints

## Old Crown
- Dense old stone town around a hill fort.
- Buildings: 28–34; 2 landmarks; high interior access.
- Combat: high close-quarters, medium verticality, many flank alleys.
- Loot: 5 dense lanes converging on fort/courtyard.
- Vehicles: 1–2 outer-road lanes only.
- Cover: walls, carts, market frames, low ruins.

## Copper Port
- Cargo yard + warehouses + cranes silhouette.
- Buildings: 20–24 large modules; 2 landmarks.
- Combat: medium CQC, strong long lanes between containers, medium verticality.
- Loot: warehouses and dock offices.
- Vehicles: 3 broad lanes and truck-access roads.
- Cover: containers, pallets, bollards, low machinery.

## Dryfield
- Sparse dry farms and irrigation lines.
- Buildings: 12–16 small compounds; 1 water-tower landmark.
- Combat: low CQC, high sightlines, terrain and field walls matter.
- Loot: farmhouse clusters rather than one hot center.
- Vehicles: 4 lanes; highest vehicle accessibility.
- Cover: hay walls, irrigation channels, stone fences.

## White Quarry
- Terraced quarry bowl with industrial sheds.
- Buildings: 14–18; 2 landmarks.
- Combat: strong vertical layers, medium-long sightlines, hard cover pockets.
- Loot: processing sheds and central machinery deck.
- Vehicles: 2 winding quarry roads.
- Cover: blocks, conveyors, loaders, cut-stone stacks.

## Redline Airstrip
- Short military-style fictional airstrip with hangars; no real insignia.
- Buildings: 12–15 large modules; 3 landmarks (tower/hangar/fuel farm).
- Combat: longest sightlines on map, dangerous runway crossing, strong hangar CQC.
- Loot: high reward in hangars/control tower.
- Vehicles: 3 straight access lanes.
- Cover: service trucks, barriers, equipment crates.

## Saltworks
- Salt pans, pump houses and raised service paths.
- Buildings: 10–14; 1 landmark.
- Combat: low verticality, mixed open water/salt-pan sightlines and tight pump-house fights.
- Loot: distributed across utility islands.
- Vehicles: 2 lanes on raised roads.
- Cover: pump blocks, piping, walls, storage sacks.

## Lantern Coast
- Cliffside fishing settlement and lighthouse district.
- Buildings: 18–22; 2 landmarks.
- Combat: medium CQC, strong elevation changes, cliff flank routes.
- Loot: houses, lighthouse store, boat sheds.
- Vehicles: 1–2 narrow coastal lanes.
- Cover: boats, stone walls, nets, terraces.

## Shared generation rules
- Layout seed = Match ID + POI ID.
- 90-degree building yaw only for reliable navigation/colliders.
- Main landmark placement stays near each POI core; standard buildings fill outer rings.
- Keep at least one readable vehicle path through every POI.
- Never procedurally block every entrance to a combat lane; retain escape routes.
- Competitive geometry should be authored from original/licensed modular assets only.
